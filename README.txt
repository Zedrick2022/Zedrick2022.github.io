# Zedrick Salupito — Agency Data Portfolio

## Files
- `index.html` — page structure, chart SVGs, certificate preview panels, contact section
- `styles.css` — responsive navy/teal/gold design and animations
- `script.js` — mobile navigation, 5-second service rotation, and contact-form mailto behavior
- `assets/` — placeholder video posters and space for your original media/PDFs

## Before publishing
1. Replace the three certificate preview panels in `index.html` with actual certificate images/links. The current designs are explicitly marked as previews, not original credentials.
2. Add your own videos to `assets/campaign-walkthrough.mp4` and `assets/creative-project.mp4`, or edit the `<source>` paths in `index.html`.
3. Add your actual PDFs at `assets/namibia-campaign-case-study.pdf` and `assets/welcome-package.pdf`, or update those links to the correct hosted URLs.
4. The campaign figures are illustrative learning data. Channel performance is listed as reviewed, but no channel-level numbers were supplied. Pie chart percentages are shares of the sum of stage counts, not unique audience shares.
5. The contact section retains the existing public page's name, email, subject, message and submit field pattern. This version prepares a mailto message; if your old form used a form provider/backend, restore its original action/endpoint before replacing the live page.

## Publish
Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, and the `assets` folder to the root of the `Zedrick2022` GitHub Pages repository. Keep the filenames and paths as provided.
