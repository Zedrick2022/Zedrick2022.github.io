01 / VOLUME

Campaign Funnel Volume

Bar chart · Total at each milestone

▥

Key Takeaway: The visual step-down highlights a potential friction point between Engagement (60,000) and Leads (18,000), where the largest absolute drop in this sample occurs. This is a signal to investigate lead capture, audience intent, and the offer—not proof of the cause.

02 / PROGRESSION

Marketing Funnel Conversion Progression

Linear / line graph · Stage trajectory

⌁

Key Takeaway: The steep decline from Reach to Leads suggests that audience volume does not translate evenly into expressed interest. The smaller decline from Leads to Conversions means two-thirds of the sample leads convert; the quality and definition of each stage should be validated before drawing business conclusions.

03 / DISTRIBUTION

Campaign Funnel Volume Distribution

Pie chart · Share of summed stage counts

◔
187,820sum of stages
Reach 52.1%Engagement 31.9%Leads 9.6%Conversions 6.4%

Key Takeaway: Reach represents 52.1% of the summed funnel-stage counts, while Conversions represent 6.4%. Because funnel stages overlap as people progress, these percentages describe the composition of the summed stage values—not independent audience shares or a standalone campaign conversion rate.
